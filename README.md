# Zelcom-Customer-Churn-Project
The project is an analysis of customer churn trends at Zelcom Ltd done using MySQL and PowerBI.

## Project Overview
Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges facing telecom companies today. High churn rates mean loss of revenue and increased acquisition costs.  
This project analyzes **Zelcom Telecom’s customer churn data (2019–2024)** to uncover key risk factors, build churn risk scoring, and recommend actionable strategies for retention.

---

## Objectives
- Measure and track churn trends across customer segments  
- Identify **drivers of churn** (demographics, contract type, billing, services)  
- Segment customers by **risk levels** to guide sales and marketing efforts  
- Provide data-driven insights for **Sales, Marketing, and Finance teams**  

---

## Key Insights
- **Churn Rate:** 27% across the 6-year period  
- **High-Risk Groups:**  
  - Senior citizens  
  - Male customers  
  - Customers on **month-to-month contracts**  
  - Customers without internet service  
- **Revenue Link:** Customers with lower monthly charges tend to churn more  
- **Seasonality:** November shows the highest churn peaks  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Used
- **Power BI** → Interactive dashboards, KPI cards, DAX (risk scoring)  
- **SQL** → Data cleaning, transformations, joins  

---

## Deliverables
- **Power BI Dashboard** (3 pages with KPIs & visuals)  
- **Executive Report** summarizing findings & recommendations  

---

## Recommendations
- **Sales:** Focus on upselling annual contracts to reduce churn in month-to-month subscribers  
- **Marketing:** Target retention campaigns at high-risk demographics (senior citizens, low-spend users)  
- **Finance:** Evaluate pricing strategies for low-charge customers to increase loyalty without hurting margins
